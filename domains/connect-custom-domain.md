# How to Connect Your Custom Domain with FORLESS

Connect your custom domain in just a few simple steps and get your website live in minutes.

---

## Step 1 — Enter Your Domain

Enter your custom domain (for example, **yourdomain.com**), then click **Connect Domain**.

---

## Step 2 — Start the Setup

Click **Let's Get Started**.

---

## Step 3 — Copy Your DNS Records

Copy the DNS records provided by **FORLESS**.

We'll add them to your domain provider in the next step.

---

## Step 4 — Open Your Domain Provider

We'll use **Namecheap** in this guide, but the same steps apply to most domain providers, including:

- Namecheap
- GoDaddy
- Cloudflare
- Hostinger
- Porkbun
- Squarespace Domains
- And many others

Choose the domain you want to connect.

Click **Manage**, then **Advanced DNS**.

---

## Step 5 — Add Your DNS Records

Click **Add New Record**.

Most domain providers follow a similar process.

### Record 1

**Type:** A

**Host / Name:** @

**Value / Points To:** 76.76.21.21

**TTL:** Automatic

Save the record.

### Record 2

**Type:** CNAME

**Host / Name:** www

**Value / Points To:** cname.forless.ai

**TTL:** Automatic

Save the record.

When you're done, return to **FORLESS** and click **I've added the records**.

---

## Step 6 — Verify Your Domain

FORLESS will verify your DNS records and connect your domain automatically.

This usually takes just a few seconds.

If your domain isn't verified yet, wait a minute, then click **Check Again**.

---

## Step 7 — Check the Connection Status

You can check your connection status anytime from your dashboard.

After your domain is verified, the connection usually completes within a few minutes to an hour. In some cases, it can take up to **24 hours**.

📧 You'll receive an email as soon as your domain is successfully connected.

---

## Your Website Is Live

That's it.

Your website is now live on your custom domain.
